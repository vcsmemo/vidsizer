The 8 MB myth

If you search "Discord video limit", half the results still say 8 MB. That was the free limit years ago. It then became 10 MB, and since August 2026 it is 20 MB. Guides quoting 8 MB — and compressor tools with an "8 MB for Discord" preset — are simply out of date.

Why does this matter? Because every megabyte you compress away unnecessarily is quality you lose for nothing. A clip squeezed to 8 MB when 20 MB would have uploaded fine looks dramatically worse. Always compress to your actual cap, not a number from a 2021 tutorial.

What counts toward the limit

  • File size, not duration. A 10-second 4K screen recording can exceed 20 MB; a 5-minute 480p clip might fit. Megabytes are all Discord checks.
  • The whole attachment. Audio track included — a 128 kbps audio track on a 3-minute video is ~3 MB of your budget.
  • 20.1 MB fails. The cap is a hard cutoff, which is why aiming for "about 20 MB" is risky and targeting ~19 MB is smart.
  • Server boosts don't raise it. Upload limits come from your plan, not the server's boost level.

FAQ

What is Discord's max video upload size?

20 MB for free accounts, 50 MB for Nitro Basic, 500 MB for full Nitro. Last verified September 2026.

Is Discord's limit still 8 MB?

No — 8 MB is years out of date. The free limit is 20 MB since August 2026.

Can I upload a video longer than the limit by splitting it?

Yes — many people split long clips into parts, but compressing to fit is usually simpler and keeps everything in one message.

Does Nitro Basic's 50 MB apply to videos?

Yes, the attachment size cap applies to any file type, including video.
