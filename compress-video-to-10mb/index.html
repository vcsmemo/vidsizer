Why 10 MB?

From 2021 until August 2026, Discord's free upload limit was 10 MB (now 20 MB, verified September 2026) — so a decade of guides, bots, and forum posts still say “under 10 MB”. Plenty of upload forms and email-adjacent workflows use 10 MB as their cap too.

What fits: roughly 30 seconds at 1080p or about a minute at 720p with clean quality. If your clip is actually for Discord today, target 20 MB instead and double your quality budget.

No — Discord raised the free limit to 20 MB in August 2026. Use 10 MB only when a specific form, bot, or guide asks for it; otherwise target 20 MB for double the quality.
