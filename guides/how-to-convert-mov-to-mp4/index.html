How to convert MOV to MP4

Your iPhone shoots MOV. Everything else wants MP4. Conversion takes one pass — here's how, and what to watch out for.

Last updated: October 2026

Why MOV won't play everywhere

MOV is Apple's QuickTime container and the iPhone default. Outside the Apple world, support is patchy: some Android phones, browsers, and upload forms refuse .mov outright, and social platforms often ask for MP4.

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio is the universal format — it plays on every phone, every browser, and every platform. Conversion is about compatibility, not quality.

What to watch out for

Only the main video and audio track carry over — extra audio tracks and subtitles don't. Keep the MOV as your archive and use the MP4 for sharing.

And convert once, from the original. Converting an already-converted file again just loses quality for no reason.
