How it works on Android

Tap browse files above and pick a video from your gallery or file manager. Choose a target size — 20 MB is a safe default for sharing — and hit Compress. The MP4 downloads straight to your Downloads folder, ready to share on WhatsApp, Telegram, or anywhere else.

One tip: keep Chrome in the foreground while it encodes, since Android can throttle background tabs. Encoding uses your phone's own CPU — that's the whole reason nothing gets uploaded.

FAQ

Does this work in Chrome on Android?

Yes. Open this page in Chrome, tap browse files, and pick a video. No app install, no permissions beyond file access — it runs entirely in the browser tab.

Where does the compressed video go?

Chrome saves the MP4 to your Downloads folder. From there you can share it to WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail, or any other app.

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. Compression runs on your phone's own chip via WebAssembly. Your video never leaves your device.
