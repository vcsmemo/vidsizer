Why extract audio from video?

A lot of video is watched for its audio: lectures, podcast recordings, talks, interviews. An MP3 lets you listen on a commute, at the gym, or while doing something else — without the video file eating your storage.

Audio is also dramatically smaller. A one-hour talk can be hundreds of megabytes as video and around 80 MB as a 192 kbps MP3 — small enough to keep, send, and play on any device.

This page is also the MP4-to-MP3 tool: MP4, MOV, WebM, and MKV inputs all work the same way. If you only need the sound, you don't need video software at all.
