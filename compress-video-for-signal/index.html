The private way to shrink a video

Most “compress video online” tools make you upload your footage to their servers first. If you care enough to message on Signal, that tradeoff makes no sense. VidSizer runs the encoder (FFmpeg, via WebAssembly) inside your browser tab — your video is never transmitted, stored, or seen by anyone.

Signal caps attachments well below what modern phone cameras shoot, so compression is unavoidable. Do it here, then send — the private part stays private end to end.

FAQ

How do I compress a video for Signal?

Drop your video above and hit Compress. Everything runs locally in your browser — pick any exact target size, get a verified file back, then attach it in Signal.

Why does on-device matter for Signal users?

Server-side compressors receive a full copy of your video. With VidSizer the bytes never leave your machine, so the privacy Signal gives your messages extends to the compression step too.

What size should I target for Signal?

20 MB is a safe default that sends quickly. If Signal still resists, step down to 10 MB or 8 MB — the tool shows its planned output before encoding.
