Why convert WebM to MP4?

WebM is Google's open format and it is excellent inside Chrome — which is why screen recorders and web tools produce it. Outside Chrome, the story is worse: iPhones and Safari have historically balked at WebM, and many social platforms and editors expect MP4.

MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio plays on every phone, every browser, and every platform. If your clip needs to travel beyond the browser, MP4 is the safe container.

FAQ

Why won't my WebM file play on iPhone?

iPhones and Safari have limited or no WebM support depending on version. Converting to MP4 (H.264 + AAC) fixes it — the same file then plays on every phone.

Will converting WebM to MP4 lose quality?

Barely, at the default 500 MB budget: the video is re-encoded with a generous bitrate, so the result is visually near-identical to the original.
