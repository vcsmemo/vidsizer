Why Messenger mangles your videos

Messenger aggressively re-encodes anything you send — a pristine 200 MB recording and a sloppy 30 MB one can arrive looking equally bad. The fix is counterintuitive: compress it yourself first, to a size Messenger accepts without a fight. You keep control of the quality-per-megabyte tradeoff instead of handing it to a chat app.

25 MB is a sensible default: light enough to send fast, heavy enough to survive one more encode on arrival.
