FAQ

How do I make a video small enough to send on Discord?

Compress it to just under your plan's cap: 20 MB (free), 50 MB (Nitro Basic), or 500 MB (Nitro). Drop your video in the tool above, pick the matching preset, and download the result — it's verified to fit before you ever try uploading.

Why does Discord keep rejecting my video?

Discord hard-rejects attachments over your plan's limit. Even 20.1 MB fails on a free account. That's why VidSizer targets ~6% under the cap instead of "about 20 MB".

Is this safe? Do you keep my video?

There's nothing to keep — compression runs entirely in your browser tab. No upload, no account, no watermark.

Will my video look bad after compression?

Short clips compress beautifully. Long clips at small targets are a physics problem, not a tool problem — VidSizer warns you upfront when the target is unrealistic, and suggests the next size up or trimming.
