FAQ

How do I compress a video to exactly 20 MB?

Select the 20 MB target above and hit Compress. VidSizer measures your clip's duration, computes the precise bitrate, and re-encodes up to 3 times until the output is provably under 20 MB.

Will a 20 MB video look good?

For clips up to a few minutes, yes — typically clean 720p. For very long clips the math gets tight, and the tool will warn you honestly before encoding.
